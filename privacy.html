English

Pipa Tuner Pro is designed to work without an account. It does not collect, store, or upload personal data.

Microphone

The microphone is used only to measure pitch while you tune. Audio is analyzed on this device. It is not recorded, saved, or sent to any server.

On this device

Tuning history, string-change notes, language, A4 reference, and tolerance stay in this device’s local storage. They are not synced or uploaded.

Purchase

The app is a one-time paid download. Apple processes the payment. We do not operate a separate account or subscription database. Buying the app includes every feature.

Tracking

The app does not track you, does not show ads, and does not include analytics SDKs.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact information on the support page.
